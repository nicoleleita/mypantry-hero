How it works

Three steps to a full week of meals

No more staring at a full pantry with no idea what to cook. My Pantry Hero does the thinking for you.

1

Stock your pantry

Add the ingredients you already have across your pantry, fridge and freezer — organised into 20 easy categories, with quick search and availability tracking.

2

Set your preferences

Choose how many days you're planning for, how many people you're feeding, your maximum cook time, and your balance of meat, vegetarian and vegan meals. Add custom requests like "Mediterranean style" or "no spicy food".

3

Generate & cook

Tap Generate and get a complete meal plan built around your ingredients — full recipes with quantities, step-by-step methods, cook times and servings. Save your favourites, add photos, and share with friends.
